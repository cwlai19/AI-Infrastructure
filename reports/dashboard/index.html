How the numbers are built. Each facility is resolved from sourced statements in the tracker (latest date, then strongest source). A facility that others are part of is listed but not summed, so the same debt is not counted twice. Amounts are commitments, converted to USD at ECB reference rates dated in the tracker's fx file. Region is the financed asset's country where the facility is secured on a site, else the borrower's, else the group's headquarters. Facilities without a signing date count in the totals but not on the timeline.

What is missing. Private loans that are neither filed nor registered do not appear until they are added from another source; detected financings show that a deal happened, not its size or lenders.
