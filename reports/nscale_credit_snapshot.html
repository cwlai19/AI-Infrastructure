Nscale: Credit Snapshot

Facts as of 29 October 2026 · Built from public primary sources: Nscale S-1 and exhibits (filed 18 Sept 2026), lender and counterparty SEC filings, UK Companies House, Anthropic S-1 (26 Oct 2026), CCIR · Prepared with AI-assisted extraction; analyst review pending
Bottom line
  • The credit risk is delivery, not leverage. Borrowed debt on the balance sheet was only $137M at 30 June 2026. The real obligations are $6.5B of customer prepayments that must be repaid in delivered compute, $2.6B of leases, and $24B of GPU orders not yet delivered, against $1.5B of cash.
  • Borrowing has accelerated since June: $830M drawn on the $900M revolver, a further $329M on the GPU loan, a $3.1B convertible (incl. $1.0B from NVIDIA), and $4.17B of new facilities.
  • Revenue is concentrated and largely future: $103.4B of contracted value, but only $2.6B is active today. Microsoft (up to $43.8B) and Anthropic (up to $44.6B) are most of it.
  • Anthropic pays only on delivery, and the financing to deliver is not in place. Nscale must fund the GPUs and the Monarch (West Virginia) campus first; Anthropic can drop late tranches.
  • Near-term refinancing point: ~$2.54B of short Dell bridge financing (3–4 month terms from Aug/Sept 2026) is meant to be taken out by the Ward County and North Carolina loans, which can only be drawn once strict conditions are met.

4. What lenders and investors are marking

  • Loans at par. PIMCO's Dynamic Income Fund carries $18.9M of a floating-rate Nscale loan at 100.02% of par (8.66% coupon, 30 June). Blue Owl's three funds hold ~$397M of commitments to the GPU Financing Facility (only ~$14M funded at 30 June).
  • Equity marks diverge. Blue Owl values Series B preferred at $16.05/share (30 June); Fidelity values the same class at $20.94 (31 July). Series C was issued at $20.39 in March 2026.
  • Lenders are also shareholders. Blue Owl funds hold both the GPU loan and Nscale preferred equity; NVIDIA is a customer, guarantor, shareholder and convertible holder. Relevant to behaviour in a restructuring.
  • Even the lenders disagree on the GPU loan's maturity: Feb 2031 (Blue Owl), Aug 2032 (PIMCO), no later than Sept 2032 (S-1 terms).
