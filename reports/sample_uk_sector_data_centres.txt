## Also check (sector-wide debt is mostly in paid databases)
  - ESMA FIRDS (instrument reference data) [free]: Every bond admitted to EU trading venues with issuer LEI, maturity and coupon: find all listed bonds of a group by LEI
  - EIB project list [free]: Signed EIB loans by borrower, amount and project
  - Export credit agencies and national promotional banks [free]: Guarantees and loans for named projects: UKEF, Eksfin (NO), EKN and SEK (SE), Finnvera (FI), EIFO (DK), Bpifrance (FR), Euler Hermes for the German government, SACE (IT), CESCE (ES); KfW, CDP, ICO
  - BDC schedules of investments (10-K/10-Q) [free]: Loan-by-loan holdings of private-credit BDCs (borrower, rate, maturity, cost, fair value). Many hold European borrowers: filter EUR/GBP positions
  - LSEG LoanConnector (LPC) [subscription]: Syndicated and private loan deal records and news
  - Octus (formerly Reorg) [subscription]: Restructuring and distressed intelligence, documents, legal analysis
  - 9fin [subscription]: Leveraged finance and private credit news, covenant analysis, document library
  - PitchBook | LCD [subscription]: Leveraged loan and private credit deals, defaults, fund data
  - IJGlobal / Inframation [subscription]: Infrastructure and project finance deal databases (data-centre financings, lenders, tenors)
